Your screen. Your board.

Keep the ChessLens desktop app open. Connect the browser showing your Chess.com board once.

Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave

BrowserOpen in its address bar
Chromechrome://extensions
Edgeedge://extensions
Bravebrave://extensions
  1. Switch on Developer mode on the extensions page.
  2. Choose Load unpacked.
  3. Select the Browser-Connector folder beside ChessLens.exe. In a source checkout, select browser-extension/chromium.
  4. Refresh the Chess.com tab.

Firefox 140 or newer

  1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  2. Choose Load Temporary Add-on.
  3. Select manifest.json inside Firefox-Connector. In a source checkout, use browser-extension/firefox.
  4. Allow access to Chess.com when Firefox asks, then refresh the tab.

This GitHub release is not Mozilla-signed. The temporary Firefox connector must be loaded again after Firefox restarts.

Visual titles and sounds

Open Options in the ChessLens bubble. Switch on a custom title for yourself, your opponent, or both, then choose GM, IM, FM, CM, WGM, WIM, WFM or WCM. Switch either control off to restore that player's normal display. These badges are visible only in your own browser; they do not change accounts or credentials.

The public download includes an original celebration chime. Choose Load my WAV sound to use your own PCM WAV recording. Sound plays once after a qualifying move is actually made.

Updating? Reload ChessLens on your browser's extensions page and refresh Chess.com. The connector reads board state and draws local markers; it does not play moves or access account cookies.
